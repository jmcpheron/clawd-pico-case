# I2C spacer + v1.8 cable slots — modelled, not printed

For Jason's external I2C signing chip (Infineon OPTIGA Trust M breakout) on 4
wires (3V3, GND, SDA, SCL) leaving the non-USB end. Case frame, mm. The
numbers come from `renders/i2c-spacer/validation.json` and
`renders/v1.8/validation.json`.

## Bottom line

- **v1.8:** the v1.7 base with two 2.5 × 1.5 slots through the non-USB end
  wall, wholly below the lid joint.
  - The lid, catches and ribs are unchanged, so the v1.7 lid still fits.
  - The base changed only at the slots (checked).
- **I2C spacer:** a new 14.6 × 3.9 × 1.4 plate.
  - It sits on the Pico between the header strips, with two pegs in the Pico's
    button-end holes.
  - Two clips at the edge hold 2 wires each, in line with the slots.
  - It touches only the Pico.
- **Checks:** all model checks pass (13 for v1.8, 12 for the spacer), and the
  browser viewer passes with 8 parts.
- **Provisional:** the Pico hole positions are datasheet numbers, the header
  strips are a render envelope, and the wire size is a 1.0 mm design
  envelope.

## Layout

- **Spacer:**
  - x 5.90–20.54 (0.30 inside the header-strip envelope); y 0.08–4.00;
    z −14.26 to −12.86.
  - 2.2 below the LCD socket bottoms and 10.9 below the LCD back.
  - Pegs Ø1.8 × 1.63 with a 0.3 chamfer, at (7.52, 2.53) and (18.92, 2.53).
  - Clips are 2.2 wide and 1.2 long, centred at x 7.40 and 19.04. They are cut
    through the plate, so the wires rest on the Pico face.
  - A 0.85 mouth on the top 0.4 snaps each wire in: push one wire in, slide it
    aside, then push in the next.
- **Slots:** x 6.15–8.65 and 17.79–20.29, z −14.51 to −13.01.
  - Through the full 3.0 wall.
  - Roof 0.65 below the seam.
  - 1.57 clear of the end catch on each side.
- **Print:**
  - The spacer goes top face down with pegs up; nothing points down above the
    bed (checked).
  - The base goes floor down; each slot roof is a 2.5 mm bridge.

## Confidence

- **High (model):** the slots don't touch the catches, ribs, lid or pry slot.
  They come straight from the case source and are checked by diffing against
  v1.7, and the seated-lid checks were re-run.
- **Medium:** the spacer stays clear of the LCD and its sockets. The 12.29 gap
  is derived from caliper rows, but L9 (parts on the LCD back) is unmeasured.
  Clearance there is large (2.2 to the sockets, 10.9 to the board).
- **Low until measured:**
  - Peg positions and hole size come from the Pico 2 W datasheet, not the PINK
    board (PINK-P20 pending), and the Pico's position under the LCD is assumed
    centred (L8 pending).
  - The header strips use the provisional H1 envelope (PINK-STRIP pending).
  - The wire size is a design envelope, not a measurement (Q-OD pending).
- **Printer-dependent:** the peg fit (0.15 per side) and the 0.85 clip mouth.
  Jason's printer makes small holes about 0.2 tighter than Austin's (J20-JM),
  and pegs and lips are new features on either printer.

## Open questions

1. **How do the wires reach the clips?** The solder joints are on the
   underside, but the clips sit on the LCD-facing face. There is only about
   0.8 between the Pico's non-USB edge and the wall, which isn't enough to
   bring a wire round the board. The wires are independent of this part, but
   their path needs deciding: bare pin above the header plastic (if any is
   exposed), another solder point, or a different clip height.
2. **Do the wires bend too sharply into the clips?** Wires coming over the
   plate step down 1.4 into the clip, which is fine for flexible 28–30 AWG
   wire. If the cable is stiff, the plate can be thinned or the clip
   lengthened.
3. **Does the spacer stay put?** The pegs are a locating fit and nothing is
   above the plate for 10.9. The wires in the clips and slots should hold it;
   if it rattles, tighten the pegs one step.
4. **Do four wires fit one slot?** If the wires are ≤ 0.8 mm, one 4-wire slot
   on one side of the catch is possible (V1.8-WHY-TWO).
5. **Do the slot roofs print clean?** Each roof is a 2.5 mm bridge 0.65 below
   the seam; check after the first print.

## External holder options (not built; Jason to pick)

- **H1, end-cap pod:** a short sleeve that slides over the non-USB end, with
  the breakout in a pocket against the end face. The pigtail plug mates
  inside the pod, so the cable and chip are fully covered.
  - Adds roughly the breakout thickness plus 2–3 mm to the case length.
  - Grips the end's outer corners, so the case itself needs no change.
- **H2, under-floor sled:** a thin tray that clips onto the base's floor, with
  the breakout lying flat under the case. The cable folds around the end edge
  into the slots.
  - The case doesn't get longer, but it gets 2–4 mm thicker.
  - The breakout's connector side has to face the non-USB end.

Both need OPT-1 and Q-PLUG first.
